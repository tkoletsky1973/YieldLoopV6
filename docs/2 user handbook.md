# Doc 02: The User Handbook
### The "Plain English" Guide to YieldLoop — From Onboarding to Exit
**Version 5.2 — Effective Feb 2026**

---

## 1. What is YieldLoop?
YieldLoop is a non-custodial protocol that puts your digital dollars (USDT) to work in the crypto market. Unlike a bank or a centralized exchange, **you own the keys.**

* **Your Vault:** A digital safe on the blockchain that only you can open.
* **Profit-Only Fees:** We only take a cut if you make money. Your original deposit is never touched by fees.
* **The Ratchet:** Our fee starts at 12% and is hard-coded to only go down as the protocol grows.

---

## 2. Getting Started (The Dry Dock)
### Step 1: Secure Your Wallet
You don’t need an existing crypto wallet. You can create one using your email or social login via **Privy**. 
* **The Master Key:** You will be given a 12-word recovery phrase. You must write this down. To finish setup, you must type back two random words from your phrase to prove you have it secured. **If you lose this phrase, no one—not even YieldLoop—can recover your funds.**

### Step 2: Funding
Once your wallet is ready, you can purchase **USDT (Tether)** directly through our integrated partner, Transak, or transfer it from another exchange. A minimum of **$1,000 USDT** is required to activate your first vault.

---

## 3. The AI Onboarding (The Bridge)
After your deposit, you don't just "buy" a strategy. You talk to the AI.

1.  **The Conversation:** Tell the AI your goals in plain English (e.g., "I want steady growth but I'm nervous about big drops").
2.  **Strategy Recommendation:** The AI will suggest a tier (Conservative, Balanced, or Growth) and show you **Kitchen Table Outcomes**—real-world dollar estimates of what you could earn and what a "bad month" looks like.
3.  **The Parameter Pre-Check:** Before you commit, the AI opens a **Strategy Screen**. It shows you exactly where every dollar is going—how much is in staking, how much is in the "Arb" pool, and what the stop-loss triggers are.
4.  **Manual Override:** If you’re an advanced user, you can tweak any of these settings. If not, the AI’s "Best Fit" is pre-filled for you.

---

## 4. Activation (The "Signed in Blood" Step)
To ensure you understand the risks, you must pass the final inspection:
* **The 10-Box Check:** You must individually check 10 boxes confirming you understand taxes, non-custodial reality, and market risk.
* **The Phrase:** You must manually type the phrase **"I UNDERSTAND AND ACCEPT RESPONSIBILITY"** to activate the trades.

---

## 5. Understanding Your Fees
Your fee starts at **12% of profits**. It drops in two ways:
1.  **AUM Drops:** As the total money in the whole YieldLoop protocol grows, the fee drops for everyone (e.g., from 12% to 11% at $1M total deposits). These drops are permanent.
2.  **Accelerator:** Your individual vault earns a "personal discount" of up to **2 percentage points** based on how consistently you add to your vault and keep your balance healthy.

---

## 6. What is LOOP?
When your vault earns a profit, you can choose to take it in USDT (cash) or **LOOP**.
* **Value:** LOOP is issued at $1.00.
* **The Growth:** Every 90 days (minimum), the protocol "Ratchets." This permanently increases the value of all LOOP you hold (e.g., from $1.00 to $1.02).
* **Safety:** Once LOOP is issued in a previous month, its value is protected.

---

## 7. Exiting the Protocol
You can leave at any time. You have two choices:
1.  **Standard Exit (No Cost):** Your positions close naturally. This can take up to 48 hours for certain "Arb" pool funds.
2.  **Immediate Exit:** You get your money now. You pay the network friction costs plus a **20% urgency fee** on those costs.
* **Redemption at Par:** If you exit in the middle of a month, your LOOP for *that month* is redeemed at **$1.00** (you keep your earnings but lose the loyalty bonus). All LOOP from *previous months* is redeemed at its full, ratcheted value.

## 8. LoopLab: The Mission
10% of all YieldLoop fees and NFT sales fund **LoopLab**. 
* **The Goal:** Providing free high-tech training (Drones, Robotics, Coding) and "Second Chance" opportunities to underserved communities, starting in the DelMarVa region.
* **Impact:** When you use YieldLoop, you are directly funding an R&D hub that turns people with "nothing" into high-value technical assets for their local community.
