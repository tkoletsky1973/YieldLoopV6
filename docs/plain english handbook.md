Doc 02: The User Handbook
The "Plain English" Guide to YieldLoop — From Onboarding to Exit
Version 6.0 — Effective Feb 2026
1. What is YieldLoop?
YieldLoop is a non-custodial protocol designed to put your digital dollars (USDT) to work in the cryptocurrency market to generate yield. Unlike traditional financial institutions, you maintain absolute control because you own the cryptographic keys.
 * Your Vault: A private digital safe on the blockchain accessible only to you.
 * Profit-Only Fees: Fees are charged only on realized profits; your original deposit is never touched.
 * The Ratchet: The platform fee begins at 12% and is hard-coded to only ever decrease as the protocol grows.
2. Getting Started
Step 1: Secure Your Wallet
You can create a digital identity using your email or social login via Privy.
 * The Master Key: You are provided with a 12-word backup phrase, which is the only way to recover access to your vault.
 * Verification: To complete the setup, you must manually type back two randomly selected words from your phrase to confirm it is secured. YieldLoop cannot recover your wallet for you.
Step 2: Funding
The protocol operates using USDT, a digital dollar pegged 1:1 with the US Dollar.
 * Buying USDT: Purchase USDT directly within the app using a bank card or transfer through our integrated partner, Transak.
 * Minimums: A minimum initial deposit of $1,000 USDT is required to activate your first vault.
3. The AI Experience (The Bridge)
When you deposit, you engage in a conversational experience with the YieldLoop AI to set your strategy.
 * Goal Discovery: You tell the AI your financial objectives in plain language.
 * Kitchen Table Outcomes: The AI recommends a risk tier (Conservative, Balanced, or Growth) and provides real-world dollar estimates of potential earnings and "worst-case" scenarios.
 * Strategy Pre-Check: The AI populates a Strategy Screen showing the exact allocation across strategies like staking, lending, and trading.
 * Manual Override: Advanced users can customize these settings before final authorization.
4. Activation (The "Signed in Blood" Step)
To ensure full understanding of the protocol, users must pass a final verification:
 * The 10-Box Check: You must individually check 10 boxes confirming your understanding of risks, taxes, and non-custodial responsibility.
 * The Confirmation: You must manually type the phrase "I UNDERSTAND AND ACCEPT RESPONSIBILITY" to activate your vault.
5. Understanding Your Fees
The fee starts at 12% of profits and decreases through two distinct mechanisms:
 * Protocol Milestone Drops: As total deposits in YieldLoop hit specific targets (e.g., $1M, $5M), the fee drops for all users permanently.
 * The Accelerator: Your individual vault earns a personal discount of up to 2 percentage points based on the consistency and quality of your activity.
6. What is LOOP?
Profits can be taken in LOOP, an internal loyalty credit that appreciates over time.
 * How it Raises: LOOP starts at $1.00 each. If the protocol's safety pools are healthy, a "Ratchet" event permanently increases the value of all outstanding LOOP.
 * The 90-Day Rule: A Ratchet event must fire at least once every 90 days, but only if the protocol's pools are healthy and above their floor levels.
 * The Math: The value of your LOOP is calculated by multiplying its starting value by every Ratchet event that has happened since you got it.
 * Redemption: You can redeem LOOP for USDT at its current grown value at any time.
 * Safety: LOOP earned in completed past months is always safe and cannot be taken away.
7. Leaving the Protocol
You can exit the protocol whenever you choose with two primary options:
 * Standard Closure: Positions wind down naturally over 5–7 days with no extra fees.
 * Immediate Exit: You receive your funds immediately, paying actual friction costs plus a 20% urgency fee.
 * Redemption at Par: On an immediate exit, LOOP earned in the current incomplete month is redeemed at its base $1.00 value, while all past LOOP retains its full grown value.
8. LoopLab: The Mission
10% of all YieldLoop fees and NFT sales fund LoopLab, an independent organization focused on technology access.
 * The Goal: Providing free high-tech training (Drones, Robotics, Coding) for underserved communities, starting in the DelMarVa region.
 * The Opportunity: LoopLab provides "Second Chance" pathways for people to build high-value technical careers.
